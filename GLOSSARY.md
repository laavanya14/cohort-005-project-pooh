# Glossary

The canonical language for Cadence. When several words exist for one concept, the term here wins and the rest are listed under _Avoid_.

## People

**Student**:
A user with the student role: someone who buys and takes courses.
_Avoid_: Learner, customer, user (when you mean a student)

**Buyer**:
The user who made a purchase: a student buying a course for themselves, or a team admin buying a team licence.
_Avoid_: Customer, purchaser, payer

**Seat holder**:
A student who got access to a course by claiming a seat. They have no purchase of their own, so they never make a refund request.
_Avoid_: Team member, redeemer

**Platform admin**:
A user with the admin role: Cadence staff who run the platform.
_Avoid_: Admin (on its own), staff, superuser

**Team admin**:
The member of a team who bought its team licences and hands out their seats. Not staff, and has no platform admin powers.
_Avoid_: Admin (on its own), team owner, manager

## Teams

**Team licence**:
A purchase a team admin makes of several seats of one course at once. Its amount paid covers all its seats.
_Avoid_: Team purchase, bulk purchase, license (spelling)

**Seat**:
One place on a team licence, which one person can claim to get access to the course. A seat is available, claimed, on hold or void.
_Avoid_: Coupon, licence (that is the whole purchase)

**Seat link**:
The link a team admin shares so someone can claim a seat.
_Avoid_: Coupon code, redeem link, invite

**Claimed**:
A seat someone has used to get access to the course.
_Avoid_: Redeemed, taken, used

**On hold**:
An available seat that can't be claimed because its team licence has an open refund request.

**Void**:
A seat that can never be claimed, because its team licence's refund request was approved.

## Refunds

**Purchase**:
A buyer's payment for a course: either their own access to it, or a team licence.

**Amount paid**:
What the buyer was actually charged for a purchase, after any discount.
_Avoid_: Price (that is the course's list price, a different thing)

**Course progress**:
A student's hours watched and lessons completed in a course, counted across all their purchases of it.
_Avoid_: Completion, usage

**Refund window**:
The period after a purchase during which a refund request can be made.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, cooling-off period

**Refund eligibility**:
Whether a purchase can have a refund request made right now.

**Refund request**:
A buyer's ask to have the amount paid for a purchase given back. It always belongs to exactly one purchase.
_Avoid_: Reversal, cancellation, chargeback, refund (on its own)

**Open**:
The status of a refund request the team hasn't decided yet, and the buyer hasn't withdrawn. A request a reviewer has looked at but not decided is still open.
_Avoid_: Pending (that word belongs to refunds), requested

**Approved**:
The status of a refund request a reviewer has accepted. Only an approved refund request produces a refund.

**Declined**:
The status of a refund request a reviewer has turned down.
_Avoid_: Rejected, denied

**Withdrawn**:
The status of a refund request the buyer closed themselves while it was open.
_Avoid_: Cancelled

**Reviewer**:
A platform admin deciding a refund request.
_Avoid_: Resolver, admin (when you mean the person deciding a refund request)

**Decision note**:
The reviewer's explanation of their decision on a refund request.
_Avoid_: Reason (that is the buyer's), resolution

**Refund**:
The money given back to the buyer's card after a refund request is approved.
_Avoid_: Reversal, chargeback

**Pending refund**:
A refund that has been issued but hasn't reached the buyer's card yet.
_Avoid_: Pending refund request (a refund request is open, never pending), on its way

**Refundable amount**:
How much a refund for a purchase would be: always the amount paid.
_Avoid_: List price

**Refunded purchase**:
A purchase whose refund has been issued.
